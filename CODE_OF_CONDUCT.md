# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors and maintainers — pledge to make participation in qship a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and assuming good faith.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users.
- Prioritising getting a contributor's fix merged over style nits.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and any
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
maintainer at **ahmedawny.one@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to respect the
privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project's
leadership.
